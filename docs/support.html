About

ParkMark is a GPS-powered parking assistant that helps you remember where you parked your car. Save your location, take a photo, leave a note, and navigate back when you are ready to leave.

Frequently Asked Questions

I saved a parking record but it is not showing on the map.

Make sure location permission is granted. Go to Settings > Privacy > Location Services > ParkMark and select "While Using the App".

My reminder notification did not arrive.

Check that notifications are enabled. Go to Settings > Notifications > ParkMark and turn on notifications. Also verify that "Parking reminders" is enabled in the app's Settings screen.

How many parking records can I save?

Free accounts allow up to 5 parking records. With a ParkMark Pro subscription, you can save unlimited records.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iOS: go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > ParkMark. On Android: open Google Play > Account > Subscriptions and cancel from there.

I purchased a subscription but premium features are not unlocked.

Tap "Restore purchases" on the app's Settings screen. If the issue persists, please contact us.

How do I delete my account?

Use the "Delete My Account" button at the bottom of the Settings screen inside the app. All your data will be permanently deleted.

Which languages does the app support?

Turkish and English. You can switch from the Settings screen or using the language button on the login screen.
